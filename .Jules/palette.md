## 2025-05-18 - Keyboard-Accessible Empty States
**Learning:** Empty states for search results require an explicit, keyboard-accessible reset path. A static "No results" message traps keyboard users who must reverse-tab out of the content area to clear their search.
**Action:** Always include a 'Clear search' button/link in empty states that uses standard focus indicators (`focus-visible:ring focus-visible:ring-sky-600`) to provide an immediate escape hatch.
